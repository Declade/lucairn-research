# Medical safe terms — prevents false-positive PII detection on clinical content.
# One term per line. Case-insensitive matching. Lines starting with # are comments.
#
# DESIGN PRINCIPLES (after v1 regression — 2026-05-22):
# 1. Multi-character (>= 5 chars preferred) unambiguous medical brand/eponym/term ONLY.
# 2. NO short common English words (Head, Soft, Closed, etc.) — the "any word in span"
#    matcher (presidio_scan.py:366) makes those over-broad and tanks recall on real PII.
# 3. NO bare abbreviations < 4 chars (IV, MD, GU, etc.) — same reason.
# 4. NO inflections of common verbs ("Reviewed", "Improved") — same reason.
#
# Goal: reduce FPs on eponymous medical brands (Foley, Marcaine, Babinski) without
# touching real PII detection.
